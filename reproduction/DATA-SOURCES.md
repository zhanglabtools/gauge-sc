# Data sources and terms

The archive contains GAUGE analysis and plotting tables. Original expression
matrices, donor metadata, and the Bruker metadata CSV are obtained from the
providers. Study attributions and applicable source terms are listed below.
The Allen-derived materials are provided for noncommercial research under the
applicable source terms. Each provider's terms apply to that provider's
materials. The archive carries no blanket license for third-party data.
